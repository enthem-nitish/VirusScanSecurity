Proactive Android Defense Guidelines

Essential steps to prevent unauthorized surveillance and malware persistence.

🔒

Disable "Install Unknown Apps"

Never leave sideloading permissions enabled permanently for browsers or file managers. Turn off immediately after testing trusted APKs.

👁

Audit Accessibility Privileges

Malware frequently requests Accessibility permissions to log keystrokes, intercept screen taps, and automate background approvals.

📩

Restrict SMS / Notification Access

Banking trojans read incoming two-factor authentication (2FA) OTP codes through notification listener and SMS read permissions.

📜

Verify Signer Fingerprints

Before installing updates from third-party channels, inspect the SHA-256 certificate fingerprint using VirusScan Security to verify provenance.

🛡

Keep Google Play Protect Enabled

Keep runtime verification active on your handset to complement pre-installation static analysis conducted through our platform.

🌐

Examine Embedded C2 Endpoints

Always inspect outbound URLs in unknown APKs to detect hardcoded Command & Control domains or unencrypted HTTP transmissions.
